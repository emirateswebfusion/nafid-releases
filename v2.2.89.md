## Purchase bill payment and drawer synchronisation

- Switching purchase bill payment mode from Cash to Credit removes linked cash transactions, restores the cash drawer balance, reconciles the drawer session, and posts debit to the supplier credit ledger.
- Transitioning from Credit to Cash soft-deletes prior supplier ledger rows, recalculates supplier balances, inserts cash transaction movements, and reconciles the cash drawer.
- Editing cash purchase bill totals adjusts linked cash register transactions and drawer balances with automated session reconciliation.
- Deleting purchase bills cleans up linked cash transactions, refunds the drawer balance, and synchronises the drawer session.
- Purchase bill mutations immediately broadcast data update events across all open POS windows.

If an older installation stalls while updating, close Nafid normally and run the full installer once. Keep the existing shop database; do not uninstall or delete local data.
